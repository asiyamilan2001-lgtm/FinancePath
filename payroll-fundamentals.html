PAYROLL PATH • MODULE 01

Payroll Fundamentals

Learn the foundations of payroll and understand how an employee's pay is calculated and processed.

🎯 Learning Objectives

By the end of this module, you should be able to:

  • Explain what payroll is and why it is important.
  • Understand the basic payroll process.
  • Identify the main types of employee pay.
  • Explain the difference between gross pay and net pay.
  • Recognise common payroll terminology.

What is Payroll?

Payroll is the process an organisation uses to calculate and pay employees for the work they have completed.

Payroll involves more than simply paying wages. Employers must calculate earnings, deductions, taxes and other contributions accurately before paying employees.

The Payroll Cycle

A typical payroll process follows a series of steps. Although the exact process can vary between organisations, the basic principles remain similar.

01

Collect Information

Gather hours worked, salaries, overtime, bonuses and other employee information.

02

Calculate Pay

Calculate gross pay and determine the deductions that need to be made.

03

Apply Deductions

Calculate deductions such as Income Tax, National Insurance and pension contributions.

04

Pay Employees

Employees receive their net pay after the required deductions have been taken.

05

Report & Record

Payroll information is recorded and the required reports are submitted.

Gross Pay vs Net Pay

One of the most important payroll concepts is understanding the difference between gross pay and net pay.

Gross Pay

The total amount an employee earns before deductions.

Net Pay

The amount an employee actually receives after deductions.

Common Payroll Terms

PAYE

Pay As You Earn. The system used by employers to collect Income Tax and National Insurance through payroll.

Gross Pay

An employee's earnings before deductions.

Net Pay

An employee's pay after deductions.

Deduction

An amount taken from an employee's gross pay.

Payroll Run

The process of calculating payroll for a particular pay period.

Payslip

A document showing an employee's earnings and deductions.
